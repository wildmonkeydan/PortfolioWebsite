Midnight Mirage Softworks - Bugyard
Games


Bugyard is an asyncronus multiplayer game where you hatch your own "buggie" and send it off to meet other players, whilst other players buggies visit you! You get to choose what happens to them when they visit, and watch them transform...

Made in 7 days for Brackey's Game Jam 2026.2 using Godot and Firebase.
